# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the awsl community a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, design, and evidence.
- Give and accept constructive feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private prompts, transcripts, or identifying data.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, insulting comments, sustained disruption, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person represents the project in
public spaces. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, release channels, and other community communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `xhinliang@gmail.com`. Do not include
sensitive reports in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good
faith, protect the reporter's privacy where practical, and apply a response
proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Possible responses include a private correction, a formal warning, temporary
restriction from project spaces, or permanent removal. Maintainers who are the
subject of a report must not participate in deciding its outcome.
